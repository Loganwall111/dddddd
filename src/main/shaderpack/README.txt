BEYOND THE LIMITS — GRAVITATIONAL LENS SHADER

This optional Iris/OptiFine shader profile adds a restrained chromatic lens warp,
fracture filaments, and underwater refraction. The Fabric mod itself does not
require a shader loader; its animated Codeverse sky and portal visuals work
without one.

Build the ZIP with: ./gradlew shaderpackZip
Then place build/distributions/BeyondTheLimits-Iris.zip in .minecraft/shaderpacks
and select it in Video Settings > Shader Packs.
